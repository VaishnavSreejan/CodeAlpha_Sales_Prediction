Total Campaigns Analyzed: 200 market regions
Average Product Sales: 14.02k units (Min: 1.6k, Max: 27.0k)

1. CHANNEL EFFECTIVENESS & CORRELATION WITH SALES:
   - TV Advertising (r = 0.7822):
     Strongest driver of sales. Accounts for ~73% of total marketing budget.
     High-budget TV campaigns consistently produce higher sales volume.
   - Radio Advertising (r = 0.5762):
     Moderate-to-strong positive correlation. Highly cost-effective channel.
     Acts as an effective multiplier when paired with TV campaigns.
   - Newspaper Advertising (r = 0.2283):
     Weakest correlation with sales. Often collinear with Radio spend,
     providing minimal independent predictive power in multivariate models.

2. BUDGET ALLOCATION SUMMARY:
   - TV: Mean spend $147.04k | Share 73.2%
   - Radio: Mean spend $23.26k | Share 11.6%
   - Newspaper: Mean spend $30.55k | Share 15.2%

3. STRATEGIC MARKETING RECOMMENDATIONS:
   - Prioritize TV & Radio Synergy: Reallocate underperforming Newspaper budget
     towards joint TV + Radio campaigns to maximize advertising ROI.
   - Avoid Over-Investing in Print: Newspaper shows diminishing and low direct return.
   - Predictability: Regression models achieve R2 > 0.89 using these three channels,
     making advertising expenditure a reliable forecasting baseline.
